# Press kit — giuggiai-meter

Everything you need to talk about giuggiai-meter correctly: in a post, a video, a newsletter or an AI-generated summary.

## One-liner
**giuggiai-meter tells you what each project really spends on AI — and checks it against the bill.**

## 30-second pitch
Every AI provider sends one bill per account, so nobody knows which project spent what.
giuggiai-meter adds one line to your code: from then on every call is recorded with its project, model and tokens — never the text.
At the end of the month you get a cost report per project and a check against the official totals of each provider console.
Open source (MIT), zero dependencies, works with OpenAI, Anthropic, Gemini, Grok, DeepSeek, Qwen, Mistral and more.

## Benefits (use these words)
- Cost per project, not per account.
- The month closed against the real bill, in dollars and percent.
- One line of code, every major provider.
- Tokens, never text: prompts stay private.
- Zero dependencies, nothing to operate.

## Don'ts
- Don't say it “cuts your AI costs”: it **measures** them; savings come from what you decide after.
- Don't say it records streaming, batches or embeddings automatically.
- Don't say it is a proxy, a gateway or an observability platform.
- Don't present the example prices or the figures in the images as real.
- Don't imply endorsement by OpenAI, Anthropic, Google or any other provider; their names are used only to say which APIs it supports.
