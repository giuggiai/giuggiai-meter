# Contributing to giuggiai-meter

Thanks for your interest!

- **Questions and bugs**: open an issue with the templates. Answers may come from the project bot, which never promises dates or features.
- **New features**: open a feature request first. The maintainers decide what enters the roadmap.
- **Pull requests**: keep them small, with tests, and run `python -m pytest` locally. By contributing you agree your work is released under the MIT License.
- **Never include** secrets, tokens, personal data or internal hostnames in issues, logs or code.
- **Security**: do not open public issues — see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
